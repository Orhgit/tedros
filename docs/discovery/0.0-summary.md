## Verified findings

- **Demographics (CBS 2024)**: 177,600 in community; 53% Ethiopia-born; centers Center (39%) + South (24%).
- **Cities by absolute count**: Netanya leads (~12.2K).
- **Cities by community share**: Kiryat Malakhi highest (15.1%) — added to SEO focus list.
- **Mortgage program**: 600K NIS, 25 yr, 0% first 10 yr, 2% next 15 yr; 5% down payment. Lottery: ~300 families per cycle, 70 NIS branch registration. Active.
- **Urban renewal (5 priority neighborhoods)**: Kiryat Moshe (Rehovot); Ramat Eliyahu (Rishon LeZion); Dora=Ramat Yadin / Neot Shaked / Kiryat Nordau (Netanya). Government decision verified; dedicated administration with cultural-bridge mandate.
- **Yad2 / Madlan**: No public API, no documented partnerships. Scrapers exist but violate ToS — legal exposure.
- **רמ"י + government data**: ✅ Open APIs at data.gov.il, land tenders at land.gov.il/Land_Tenders, transactions via Tax Authority. Stable, legal, free.

## Strategic recommendation: government open-data first

Build the listings layer on **רמ"י tenders + Tax Authority transactions + housing programs** rather than scraping Yad2/Madlan. Community-supplied listings (broker form on our platform) form the second layer. Yad2/Madlan only enter via formal partnership.

**Why**:

- Neutralizes R1 (legal scraping risk).
- Creates moat: government data presented in HE/EN/AM with community context — no competitor does this.
- Free + legal + reliable.
