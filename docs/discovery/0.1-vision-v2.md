# Vision v2 — Comprehensive Community Portal

**Date**: 2026-04-27
**Status**: Proposal. Supersedes the narrow framing of [0.0-summary.md](./0.0-summary.md). Pending owner sign-off.
**Owner**: Vega.
**Inputs**: [research/01-organizations-map.md](../research/01-organizations-map.md), [research/02-community-needs-deep-dive.md](../research/02-community-needs-deep-dive.md), original Phase 0 summary, ADR-000, ADR-001.

## What changes vs v1

The original Phase 0 framed Tedros as **"Ethiopian-Israeli community platform: rights, professionals directory, and real estate (top priority)"** with a 7-phase roadmap that put real-estate MVP at Phase 3 and everything else at Phases 4–7.

Research surfaced two findings that force a wider lens:

1. **The community needs are wider than three pillars.** A community member touches at least seven life-domains where they hit the same wall: Hebrew-bureaucracy-tax, no Amharic-fluent service map, no aggregation of programs, no action layer over the information.
2. **Real-estate-first is _slow_ user acquisition.** The SEO field is contested. Information pillars (rights, scholarships, mortgage wizard, racism reporting, Amharic-fluent providers) acquire users faster because the SEO competition is encyclopedic (Kol-Zchut) — not action-driven.

**v2 keeps the existing real-estate pillar as the monetization spine** (per ADR-000 D2 and existing Multica work). It widens the surface to ten pillars and reorganizes the roadmap so information pillars launch _alongside_ real-estate, not after it.

## Mission (revised)

> **לא רק לדעת — לקבל. לא רק לבקש — לקבל בחזרה.**
> _Not just to know — to receive. Not just to ask — to receive back._
>
> Tedros is the unified action layer for everything an Ethiopian-Israeli community member needs across the life journey: rights, real estate, professionals, employment, education, health, family, heritage, news, and voice. We aggregate what already exists, translate it into HE/EN/AM, and turn reading into doing.

## Vision (3-year)

By end of 2029, Tedros is the **default first stop** for any community member with a life question — from "what's my urban-renewal status?" to "where do I find an Amharic-speaking pediatrician in Netanya?" to "how do I claim the 600K mortgage?" to "how do I file a complaint about police conduct?" — and Tedros is sustained by transactional revenue (real-estate leads, professional referrals, employer postings) rather than dependency on grants.

## Roadmap v2 — 9 phases

### Phase 0 — Discovery & Audit

**Status**: 90% complete.

- ✅ Demographics, govt programs verified.
- ✅ Tech stack ADR-001 accepted.
- ✅ Risk register R1–R10 + new R11–R18 (see updated [risk-register.md](./risk-register.md)).
- 🟡 Community interviews per [interview-protocol.md](./interview-protocol.md) — to be expanded beyond real-estate to cover all 10 pillars.
- 🟡 Outreach to Tene Briut + Tebeka for anchor partnerships (P6, new).

### Phase 1 — Strategy & Architecture

**Status**: In progress (skeleton landed).

- ✅ React Router v7 + Postgres + Drizzle + Auth.js skeleton.
- ✅ HE/EN/AM i18n (Paraglide) wired.
- ✅ DB schema covers identity, real-estate, rights, professionals, content, audit.
- 🟡 Schema extension for new pillars: `health.providers`, `education.scholarships`, `family.crisis_resources`, `heritage.events`, `voice.reports` — designed before Phase 2 closes.

### Phase 2 — Design System & Brand

**Status**: In progress.

- Visual language honoring Beta Israel heritage + modern action-oriented UI.
- AA+ accessibility, full RTL, Ge'ez glyph support tested.
- Component library covers wizard / form / directory / map / crisis-mode UX patterns.
- **New for v2**: crisis-mode design pattern (one-tap, anonymous, multi-language) for Voice & Family pillars.

### Phase 3 — MVP: Rights Hub + Real Estate (parallel)

**Target**: live in 6–8 weeks (revised from 5–7; widened scope).

- **Rights & Programs MVP** (Pillar 1):
  - 600K mortgage eligibility wizard + branch finder.
  - Klita basket calculator for new immigrants.
  - Public-housing waitlist info per city.
  - Education tutoring grant explainer.
  - Programmatic SEO: "(זכות) × עיר" cells.
- **Real Estate MVP** (Pillar 2):
  - Continues existing Multica branches: listings CRUD, agency dashboard, lead capture, mortgage calculator (already merged).
  - Cities × neighborhoods × programs SEO pages (already merged).
  - Government-data ingestion (רמ"י tenders, Tax Authority transactions).
- **Required before launch**: Tebeka anchor partnership signed (R12 mitigation).

### Phase 4 — Professionals Directory + Booking

**Target**: 4 weeks after Phase 3 MVP.

- Vetted directory: lawyer / broker / financial advisor / social worker / community pediatrician.
- Booking flow with calendar integration (Cal.com self-hosted, free).
- Review system with anti-fraud guardrails (verified-experience reviews only).
- Featured-listing monetization tier.

### Phase 5 — Employment & Education Hub (parallel)

**Target**: 4 weeks.

- **Employment** (Pillar 4):
  - Curated job board with employer accountability scoring.
  - Olim Beyahad / Tech-Career feed integration.
  - Cohort calendar for tech bootcamps (Tech-Career, Net4U, Coding Academy).
- **Education** (Pillar 5):
  - Scholarship aggregator (ISEF, Hesegim, university-specific) with deadline alerts.
  - Mechinot comparison.
  - Mentor matching (community-pro to youth).

### Phase 6 — Health + Family Support (parallel)

**Target**: 6 weeks (sensitive — needs partnership setup).

- **Health** (Pillar 6):
  - Amharic-fluent provider directory (anchored by Tene Briut content).
  - Mental-health resources hub (Amharic-language explainers).
  - Chronic-disease prevention content syndicated from Tene Briut.
- **Family Support** (Pillar 7):
  - Crisis-mode UX (one-tap, anonymous, multi-lingual).
  - Domestic-violence resource directory + safe-flow contact.
  - Women's empowerment events + groups.
  - Senior-care provider directory.
- **Hard requirement**: Tene Briut anchor partnership before public Health launch.

### Phase 7 — Heritage, Culture & News

**Target**: 4 weeks.

- **Heritage** (Pillar 8):
  - Sigd event calendar (national + city editions).
  - Kessim & community-synagogue locator.
  - Oral-history archive (co-publish with Heritage Center).
  - Amharic/Ge'ez learning resources.
- **Community News** (Pillar 9):
  - Aggregated news from IAEJ + ENP + Tene Briut + community press.
  - Live representation dashboard (% community in govt by ministry — pulled from State Comptroller data).
  - Events feed.

### Phase 8 — Voice & Advocacy

**Target**: 6 weeks (high-stakes, needs Tebeka co-design).

- **Voice** (Pillar 10):
  - Structured racism / police-conduct complaint flow.
  - Auto-routing: anonymous → Tebeka triage; consented → מח"ש direct.
  - Personal-story sharing with consent + anonymization controls.
  - Petition/campaign hosting infrastructure.
- **Hard requirement**: Tebeka co-design + data-security audit before launch.

### Phase 9 — Launch, Automation & Scale

**Target**: 6 weeks.

- Multica autopilot specs production-grade:
  - Govt-program-change detector (3242/3243 etc.).
  - Broken-link audit (weekly).
  - SEO ranking report (weekly).
  - Lead aging + agency response-time tracking (daily).
  - Scholarship-deadline pusher (daily).
  - Sigd-season activation (annual, ramps up 8 weeks before 29 Cheshvan).
- GlitchTip + Plausible self-hosted observability fully wired.
- Backups, runbooks, recovery testing.
- Public marketing launch.

## Monetization model (revised)

A **portfolio** instead of a single-track risk:

| Stream                                            | Pillar  | Type                                | Margin     | Risk                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ------- | ----------------------------------- | ---------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Real-estate broker leads                          | 2       | Per-lead                            | High       | R8 (unvalidated until Phase 3 pilot) |
| Real-estate listing fees                          | 2       | Subscription (transactional Stripe) | Medium     | Validates with R8                    |
| Professional referral fees / featured listings    | 3       | Per-referral or monthly             | Medium     | New                                  |
| Employer job-posting fees                         | 4       | Per-posting                         | Low-medium | New                                  |
| Sponsored content (transparent, partner-only)     | 7, 8, 9 | Flat                                | Low        | New                                  |
| Optional donations                                | All     | Voluntary                           | Low        | New                                  |
| Government tenders for community service delivery | All     | Project-based                       | Variable   | Aspirational (Phase 9+)              |

**Cost discipline (P5 unchanged)**: zero monthly subscriptions. Self-hosted Postgres, Plausible, GlitchTip, Cal.com on user's server. Free-tier Cloudflare CDN + R2 + Resend. Stripe transactional only.
