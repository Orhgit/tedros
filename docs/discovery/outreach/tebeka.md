> **1. Rights pillar (Pillar 1) — Legal triage.**

This is the same routing you already provide for 1,000+ queries a year; we just bring users to that door who otherwise wouldn't have known.

> **2. Voice pillar (Pillar 10) — Co-design of racism and police-conduct reporting.**
> This is the platform's most sensitive surface, and I won't build it without your involvement. Every decision — anonymization, routing (מח"ש vs. identity-protection), data retention, advisor flow for complainants — needs your co-design. Initial idea: a structured submission flow built with your oversight, no other way.
>
> **Tertiary, less urgent**: legal review for the rights pillar broadly. Every page in the "what to do" layer carries a clear "information, not legal advice" disclaimer, but we want a Tebeka pair-of-eyes on the core pages before they go live.
