# LinkedIn DM Templates — Founding-Cohort Professional Recruitment (RIN-468)

**Purpose**: 8 domain-specific LinkedIn DM templates for recruiting the first 5 Amharic-fluent professionals (RIN-468). Per the 2026-05-01 research brief, this is **channel #3 fallback** — primary channel is owner's warm intros, but having templates ready means the fallback runs at speed when needed.

**Use mode**:

- Owner identifies 5 candidates per domain via LinkedIn search (e.g., `(תחום) + Ethiopian + Amharic` or `(תחום) + Ethiopian-Israeli`).
- Owner cross-references with Israeli Bar / GISA / HUDA / Psychologists Council registries to verify professional status.
- Owner sends domain-template (below), personalized 1 line opener referencing recipient's profile.
- Realistic conversion: ~15–20% reply rate at this length and pitch. 40 DMs → 6–8 replies → 4–5 confirmed = the founding 5.

**Time budget**: 1 evening (~3 hours) to send 40 DMs. 1 hour/week × 3 weeks for follow-ups.

**Drafted in Hebrew only** — all 8 domains target Hebrew-fluent profs (LinkedIn primary language for white-collar IL profs is Hebrew). Amharic option available for community-organizing roles via Facebook groups (separate channel).

---

## Pitch frame (90-second pattern)

```
[1] Personalized opener (1 line, references their profile/work)
[2] What is Tedros (1 sentence)
[3] What makes their domain critical (1 sentence)
[4] The founding-cohort offer (3 bullets)
[5] One ask: yes/no + 6-min form
```

Total: 5 lines + 3 bullets ≤ 90 seconds to read.

## Search tactics on LinkedIn

1. **Boolean search**: `(תחום) AND (Amharic OR אמהרית OR Ethiopian)` — 5–15 hits per domain typically.
2. **Cross-reference with above registries** — verify professional status to avoid wasting DM on unlicensed practitioners.
3. **Target community alumni groups**: Tech-Career alumni, Olim Beyahad alumni, ENP alumni — many list profession on profile.
4. **Filter by 1st/2nd-degree connections** — owner's network mutuals first (highest conversion ~50%), then 2nd-degree (~20%), then cold (~10%).
