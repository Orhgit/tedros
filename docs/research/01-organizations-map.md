# Ecosystem Map — Organizations Serving the Ethiopian-Israeli Community

**Date**: 2026-04-27
**Author**: Vega (research synthesis)
**Status**: Phase 0.5 — input to Vision v2.

Maps the organizational landscape that already serves the community. Every Tedros pillar must understand who is already present, what they do well, where the gap is, and whether they are a partner or a competitor.

## Executive summary

- **No single comprehensive portal exists.** The closest is [Kol-Zchut's Ethiopian portal](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/יוצאי_אתיופיה) — encyclopedic, rights-only, and not transactional.
- **Vertical specialists are strong.** Tene Briut (health), Tebeka (legal), ENP (education), Olim Beyahad / Tech Career (employment), Fidel (youth) are all credible and well-funded.
- **Gap = aggregation + action layer.** Community members must visit 6–10 separate sites + Facebook/WhatsApp groups to assemble what they need. Tedros's opportunity is the unified action layer.
- **Partnerships are mandatory, not optional.** R2 (community migration) and R6 (SEO authority) are both addressed by partnering with these orgs, not bypassing them.

## Coverage gaps Tedros fills

1. **Aggregation**: No single portal where a community member can find *every* relevant resource across rights, real estate, professionals, employment, health, education, family support, heritage. Today they assemble it themselves across 6–10 sites + WhatsApp/Facebook.
2. **Action layer**: Most existing sites are encyclopedic (Kol-Zchut) or program-specific (ENP). None offer "click → file complaint", "click → book Amharic-speaking pediatrician", "click → apply for the 600K mortgage", "click → see urban-renewal status of my street."
3. **Tri-lingual day-1**: Almost every existing org site is Hebrew-only. Amharic is rare. Tedros's HE/EN/AM commitment is differentiated.
4. **Programmatic SEO**: City × topic × language. No competitor does this at the community level (Yad2/Madlan don't, ENP/Tebeka don't). This is our path to top-rank in 90 days.
5. **Trust-by-default**: A platform that *visibly* features community professionals + community-vetted programs + partnership badges from Tene Briut/Tebeka/ENP starts with trust no generic site has.

## Partnership strategy (priority order)

1. **Tier 1 — Trust anchors (must have before launch)**: Tene Briut (health), Tebeka (legal). These two carry the credibility that lets us launch rights, professionals, and health pillars without looking like another generic info site.
2. **Tier 2 — Distribution partners (Phase 4–5)**: ENP, Olim Beyahad, Tech-Career, Fidel. Co-marketing + content cross-pollination; their newsletter/events feed becomes our community calendar.
3. **Tier 3 — Heritage + religion (Phase 6+)**: Council of Kessim, Heritage Center, IAEJ. Cultural authority and Sigd-event programming.
4. **Tier 4 — Government data (silent partners)**: רמ"י, data.gov.il, Tax Authority. Already covered by ADR-001 strategy. No relationship needed; only adapter monitoring.
