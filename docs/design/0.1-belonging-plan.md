# Belonging Plan — Design Direction v2

**Date**: 2026-04-27
**Status**: Active. Builds on top of [`0.brand.md`](./0.brand.md) (Phase 2 design system, locked baseline).
**Owner**: Designer (Vega holds until Designer agent picks up).
**Inputs**: Owner direction "צבעים ותכנון שיראה שייכות" — colors and design that show belonging.

This document does _not_ rewrite the brand. The existing brand doc — earth palette + Ethiopian-flag accents (used sparingly) + Heebo/Inter/Noto Sans Ethiopic + AA+ a11y — is the foundation. This plan amplifies the **emotional layer**: the user-felt sense of _"this site was built for me, by people who know me."_

The motto already locked in is the north star:

> **"מהמקום שלך, אל המקום שלך."**
> **"From where you are, to where you belong."**
> **"ካለህበት ወደ መልክህ።"**

Everything below answers one question: how does each design choice prove that motto isn't marketing copy?

## What's on main right now (after this PR)

- ✅ Earth palette as primary surface (`--color-earth-{50…950}`); flag accents (green/red/yellow) reserved for ≤10% of UI
- ✅ Sigd ceremonial color (`--accent-sigd`, deep purple) for cultural moments
- ✅ `flag-stripe` utility — 3-color top-of-page banner used as cultural cue (now visible on home + login)
- ✅ Typography stack with Amharic-aware line-height (`html[lang^="am"] { line-height: 1.7 }`)
- ✅ Logical CSS for RTL/LTR
- ✅ Reduced-motion handling
- ✅ Focus rings, AA+ baseline

This is the _table stakes_. Belonging needs more.

## Belonging amplification — 10 layers

### 1. Photography — community is the imagery

**No stock photos. Period.**

- Source: real community members, consent-released. Initial cohort target: 12–20 portraits.
- Settings: home (coffee ceremony, kitchen), workplace, synagogue, urban-renewal sites in the 5 priority neighborhoods, schools.
- Style: documentary natural light. Eye contact. **Not** corporate-headshot, **not** smiling-at-camera-on-grass.
- Diversity within: 1st gen Ethiopia-born + 2nd gen Israel-born; men + women + non-binary; religious + secular; ages 20–80.
- Anti-pattern: ❌ "afro hair on green field" stock; ❌ children-only images patronizing adult viewers.

**First photo session**: 1 day, 5–8 subjects, photographer from the community if possible. Budget tracker in Multica issue (TED-29 Designer).

### 2. Patterns from textile heritage (subtle, never fullbleed)

Use motifs from gabi (everyday white robe) and netela (women's shawl) as **structural dividers**, not decorations:

- Geometric border between section boundaries (1–2px, single color, never patterned over content)
- Empty states: hand-drawn motif as placeholder where data isn't loaded yet
- Footer: a thin pattern strip above copyright

**Constraints**:

- No fullbleed patterns. They become noise.
- One motif per page maximum. Repetition = cheap.
- Each motif documented in tokens.json with cultural origin note.

### 3. Cultural moments visible in the calendar

The site changes seasonally, not as a gimmick — as a recognition the community lives by its calendar:

- **Sigd (29 Cheshvan)**: 7 days before, the home page banner shifts to `accent-sigd` purple. Dedicated mini-page with event calendar, location-by-city, kessim availability for ceremonies. Returns to default after Sigd.
- **Falash Mura aliyah arrivals**: when a flight lands, an opt-in "welcome new neighbors" banner with klita-center info per city.
- **Coffee ceremony references**: empty states for community pages show a small jebena (coffee pot) illustration with the message "התוכן בהכנה — כמו קפה טוב, לוקח זמן."

### 4. Micro-Amharic on Hebrew pages

Even when the user reads Hebrew, sprinkle **2–3 Amharic words per page** with hover-tooltips that show the translation. This signals "Amharic is welcome here, not segregated to /am."

Examples:

- Home page hero: small inscription **ካለህበት ወደ መልክህ** in subtle ink-300 above the Hebrew tagline.
- Greeting on dashboard: **ሰላም {name}** before the Hebrew "שלום."
- Footer farewell: **ደህና ሁን** ("be well") next to copyright.

Not a translation overlay — a co-existence.

### 5. Local references that prove "we know you"

On every city page, lead with what the community knows about the city, not generic data:

- "**12,200 בני קהילה גרים בנתניה**" (data → relatable)
- Small map highlighting community-relevant locations: synagogue, ENP center, urban-renewal project, kessim residence.
- Names of real community institutions in each city (schools with תמ"א-12, community centers).
- Recent press snippets relevant to that city's community.

This is what makes Tedros different from Yad2: Yad2 shows the city; we show _your community in that city_.

### 6. Trust signals — visible, not buried

Anchor partnerships are the single biggest belonging proof. Make them visible:

- Footer: a "Partners" ribbon with logos (Tene Briut, Tebeka, ENP, IAEJ, Heritage Center) — only when the partnership is signed; otherwise no placeholder. Honest > complete.
- Professional cards: "Verified by [Tebeka legal review]" or "Tene Briut clinician network" badges where applicable.
- Rights pages: "Sources" section at the bottom of every page (gov.il / CBS / רמ"י links). E-E-A-T from day one.
- Author byline on long-form content: real names (no "by Tedros team" hand-waving).

### 7. Voice — direct, never patronizing

Already in `0.brand.md` but worth reinforcing:

- ✅ "**אתה זכאי, וכך תפעל.**" (You are entitled, here's how to act.)
- ❌ "**האזרח עשוי להיות זכאי…**" (The citizen may be entitled…)

The community has been talked _about_ for 40 years. Tedros talks _to_ them.

### 8. Negative space + restraint

Belonging is felt when nothing screams. The current homepage has 10 pillar tiles + a hero + a subscribe block. That's a lot. Keep:

- Generous gap between sections (`mt-16` → `mt-20` for hero→pillars→subscribe)
- One CTA per section maximum
- No carousel, no auto-play, no popups, no "are you sure?" — never interrupt the user

### 9. Mobile-first (~80% of community traffic will be mobile)

- Touch targets ≥44×44px (already in shadcn baseline)
- Bottom nav variant for primary actions on mobile
- Tappable phone numbers (`tel:`) on every professional card
- WhatsApp share buttons on every page (it's how the community moves info — meet them where they are)

### 10. Crisis-mode UX for Family + Voice pillars

This is the highest-stakes design surface. Per Vision v2 §"Phase 6/8" + risk register R16/R17:

- Quick-exit button always visible (top-right on RTL, top-left on LTR).
- "Clear browser history" reminder on entry to Family/DV pages.
- No analytics on these pages (`<head>` opts out of Plausible script).
- High-contrast mode forced (no user preference override).
- Less ornamentation: no flag-stripe, no patterns. Just clarity.
- ADR-012 (to be authored) defines the data-minimization model.

## Per-pillar visual cues

Each pillar tile + landing page leans on one accent without becoming flag-y:

| Pillar             | Lead accent           | Rationale                             |
| ------------------ | --------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Rights & Programs  | `accent-green` 8%     | Growth, claim what's yours            |
| Real Estate        | `earth-500` (primary) | Home, root                            |
| Professionals      | `earth-700`           | Authority, vetted                     |
| Employment         | `accent-yellow` 10%   | Optimism, opportunity                 |
| Education          | `accent-sigd` 12%     | Future, dedication                    |
| Health             | `accent-green` 10%    | Wellness, calm                        |
| Family Support     | `earth-600` warm      | Holding, care                         |
| Heritage & Culture | `accent-yellow` 15%   | Gold of Beta Israel                   |
| News               | `ink-700` neutral     | Read first, decoration second         |
| Voice & Advocacy   | `accent-red` 5%       | Urgency, restraint — never decorative |

**Critical rule**: accent ≠ background of large surfaces. Accent = a stripe, an icon fill, a focus ring, a small badge. Never a hero background unless it's a **single Sigd-week treatment** sanctioned by the Designer + Council of Kessim.

## Anti-patterns we will _not_ ship

A list to point at when temptation arises:

- ❌ Ethiopian flag colors at full saturation as page background — flag, not portal.
- ❌ Stock-photo "diversity" headshots — community spots fake immediately.
- ❌ Sentimental/nostalgic design language ("a stick, a pot, the elder") — we are an active platform, not a museum exhibit.
- ❌ Patronizing UX ("are you sure?", over-validation, hand-holding wizards). Community has been infantilized for 40 years; we won't.
- ❌ Decorative use of the Ethiopian Cross or other religious imagery without explicit Council-of-Kessim consultation.
- ❌ Decorative Amharic text where the Hebrew/English is the real content. If it's there, it means something.
- ❌ Animated Sigd umbrellas, dancing kessim, or any imagery that feels like exoticism.

## Implementation guidance for Designer + Engineer

When extending the design:

1. **Start from tokens, not values**. Never type a hex code in a component.
2. **Logical CSS only**. `inset-inline-start` not `left`. `padding-inline` not `padding-x`.
3. **Test in 3 locales before merging**. Storybook toolbar will enforce.
4. **One source of truth**: `tokens.json` first; `app/app.css @theme` mirrors it. Component classes consume the @theme.
5. **Anti-patterns from this list go in component story `argTypes` as warning text** — engineer can't accidentally introduce them without reading the warning.
